# Original AirXonix Team — Credits and Thanks

**AirXonix** was created by **AxySoft**. This repository is an independent reverse-engineering and preservation effort, not an official AxySoft release.

- **Vladimir Savin** — programming and original game development.
- **Maksim Zozulya (McSim)** — music and sound effects.
- **Vadim Sobolev (SoWa)** — texture creation.
- **Egor Saenko (Geo Soy Weird / George Soy Weird)** — artwork.

## In memory of Vladimir Savin

With deep gratitude and respect, we remember Vladimir Savin, whose work brought AirXonix to life. His creation has delighted generations of players and continues to inspire us to preserve the game for new devices.

**Thank you, Vladimir, for your talent, dedication and enduring contribution to games.** We also extend our heartfelt respect and warmest thoughts to his family and loved ones. May his work and memory live on through the joy that these games continue to bring.
